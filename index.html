Goldor

F7 · Goldor Phase

You are the tank. Vallon's, Reborn Gold and Doggy each run to their own terminal. Yours is marked in gold, and you get 4 different terminals this run.

The terminals float over the void. You stop at the edge of a platform. Press Space (or Jump) to jump the gap, and time your jumps onto the moving platforms. Goldor's red circle is instant death.

In the boss fight your team does the damage. You hold aggro (use your Lasso to yank Goldor back), take the splash slams and heal your team.

Master Mode: Goldor's slams and shockwaves one-tap you.

Try Hard Mode: solo. 3 rooms with 3 terminals each, all yours, while a red laser eats the room behind you. Then the Master Mode boss.
