Goldor

F7 · Goldor Phase

You are the tank. Vallon's, Reborn Gold and Doggy each run to their own terminal. Yours is marked in gold, and you get 4 different terminals this run.

The terminals float over the void. Walk or click toward a platform and you hop the gap automatically when it's in reach; time your hops onto the moving platforms. Keep out of Goldor's red aura.

In the boss fight your team does the damage. You hold aggro, take the splash slams and keep everyone alive.
